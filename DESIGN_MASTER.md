# Master Prototype — Final Solo Feature Release

The Master Prototype is the final feature-complete public demonstration before organizational adoption. Future work should be either maintenance (bugs, compatibility, copy fixes) or adoption work (authentication, durable storage, approved integrations/content, security/governance).

## Design principles
1. Deepen the workspace without cluttering it.
2. Keep public interactions presentation-safe and browser-local where possible.
3. Make Atlas an interface to the workspace, not a detached chatbot.
4. Preserve one consistent hierarchy across eight libraries.
5. Make the prototype understandable on first visit through onboarding and clear boundaries.
6. Keep the codebase handoff-ready and the public Render URL stable.
