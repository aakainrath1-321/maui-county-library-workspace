Maui County Library Workspace Prototype

A shell to explore, test, and improve together.

This version demonstrates the proposed organization, design, navigation, editing experience, collaboration features, Atlas workspace intelligence, and branch identity system.

01

Why it's public

The prototype is intentionally open so staff and stakeholders can explore it easily. Real internal operational content is not included.

02

What comes later

If formally adopted, approved content, organizational authentication, role-based permissions, and production integrations can be added behind appropriate security controls.

03

What we need from you

Explore it. Try the navigation. Open Atlas. Test Edit Mode. Then tell us what is useful, confusing, unnecessary, or missing.
